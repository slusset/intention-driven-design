What It Means

Intent-Driven Design starts by identifying why users act, what they expect to accomplish, and which outcomes matter to the business.

  • User Intent: The goal behind each action.
  • Decision Clarity: Fewer choices, stronger signals.
  • Outcome Focus: Measure behavior, not vanity metrics.

1. Discover Intent

Map real motivations with interviews, journey data, and friction analysis.

2. Design for Decisions

Shape flows that reduce cognitive load and guide users to confident choices.

3. Validate Outcomes

Test whether behavior changed in the direction your strategy intended.

Intent to Impact

When intent drives design, teams ship less noise, create better alignment, and improve both user success and business performance.
